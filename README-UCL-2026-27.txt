PE League 2026 — UCL League Phase + Knockout template

UCL mode now uses a 36-team League Phase. Each team receives 8 matches (144 matches total).
Positions 1-8: direct to Round of 16
Positions 9-24: Knockout Play-off
Positions 25-36: eliminated
Play-off winners: 8 teams join the 8 seeded teams in Round of 16.
Knockout bracket: Play-off -> Round of 16 -> Quarter-final -> Semi-final -> Final.

Important: select exactly 36 clubs before creating the UCL schedule.
