PE LEAGUE - UCL CUSTOM GROUPS UPDATE

UCL League Phase now supports configurable group counts and group sizes.
- Select 4-64 teams for the UCL season.
- Choose the number of groups (up to 16, limited by 2 teams per group).
- Choose the number of teams in each group.
- The sum of all group sizes must equal the selected season roster.
- Each group must have at least 2 teams.
- Teams play home/away inside their group.
- Top 2 from each group advance to the knockout phase.
- If the number of qualified teams is not a power of two, the knockout bracket uses byes.
- Existing League and FA Cup modes are unchanged.
